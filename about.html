Pricing Methodology & Public JSON Feed

TokenLedger is an independent, open-source AI FinOps workbench designed for engineers, founders, and finance leads.

1. Rate Table Integrity

All 24 LLM rates and 6 embedding rates are stored in a single public JSON feed at /api/pricing.json (snapshot date: 2026-11-24). Standard published list prices are used for planning estimates; enterprise committed-use discounts or regional surcharges should be verified directly on each vendor’s console.

2. Client-Side Token Estimation

To keep pasted prompts 100% private with zero server calls, TokenLedger runs a deterministic multi-family estimator calibrated separately for ASCII prose, code/JSON punctuation density, and CJK/Korean UTF-8 scripts across OpenAI o200k_base, Anthropic Claude BPE, and Google Gemini SentencePiece tokenizers. Expect ±5–10% variance compared to exact server-side tokenizers.

3. Sustainability & Sponsorship

TokenLedger is free with no login wall. Selected pages feature curated AI Engineering & FinOps books with disclosed affiliate links (dopabrain-20) and open GitHub sponsorship slots.
