1. Why Peak TPM — Not Daily Average — Breaks Production AI Apps

SaaS traffic is never evenly spread across 24 hours. A morning standup burst or batch webhook can spike RPM 2.5x–4x above average, instantly exhausting Anthropic or OpenAI Tokens-Per-Minute (TPM) ceilings and triggering cascading 429 retries.

2. Sizing Concurrent Streams with Little’s Law

Required concurrent open connections equal (Peak RPM ÷ 60) × Average Latency in seconds. A 300-RPM burst with 6-second streaming responses holds 30 concurrent workers open—meaning slow frontier reasoning models amplify connection-pool pressure.

3. Designing Graceful 429/529 Failover Policies

Combine jittered exponential backoff with cross-region endpoints (e.g., US + EU Bedrock/Vertex) or an automated secondary provider fallback so users never see hard timeouts during provider congestion.
