1. Separate Perceived Latency (TTFT) From Total Generation Time

Users perceive an AI chat or voice agent as responsive when the first token streams under 500–800ms. Switching from non-streaming REST waits to Server-Sent Events (SSE) immediately cuts perceived lag by 70%.

2. Matching Inference Hardware to SLA Tier

Use ultra-fast Cerebras or Groq endpoints (300–2,100 tok/s) for voice agents and autocomplete, while reserving slower deep-reasoning models (o3, DeepSeek-R1) for async background research tabs.
