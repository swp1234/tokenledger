Why PDF & Image Billing Differs 5x Across Providers

Google Gemini bills standard images/pages at a flat ~258 tokens, whereas tile-based vision models (OpenAI high-detail, Anthropic Claude) can consume 765 to 1,600+ tokens per page depending on resolution. Resizing document scans to 1024px before upload saves 40–60% without hurting OCR accuracy.

Speech-to-Speech Realtime vs. Cascaded Voice Pipelines

Native speech-to-speech Realtime APIs deliver ultra-low latency (~300ms) at $0.04–$0.24/minute, while a cascaded Whisper STT + Flash LLM + streaming TTS pipeline runs ~$0.025–$0.035/minute for structured support calls.
