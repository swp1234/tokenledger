Traditional SaaS (85% Margin) vs. AI-Native SaaS (55–78% Margin)

Unlike classic CRUD SaaS where marginal server cost per user approaches pennies, an active AI seat running 500 frontier requests per month can easily burn $8–$20 in direct token COGS—wiping out a $20/month flat subscription once payment fees are included.

Calculating Your Hard Power-User Break-Even Cap

Every subscription tier has an exact break-even request ceiling: (Price − Payment Fee − Fixed Infra) ÷ Cost per Request. Knowing that threshold lets you design fair soft-throttles (e.g., fast frontier credits followed by automatic Flash/Mini fallback) instead of silent negative margins.

Three Pricing Levers That Protect Margin

1) Hybrid seat + pooled team credits; 2) Automatic model routing by task complexity; 3) Prompt caching on shared workspace knowledge bases.
