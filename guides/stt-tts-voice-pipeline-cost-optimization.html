1. Strip Silence Before Calling STT APIs (25–40% Instant Savings)

Unedited customer support calls and meeting recordings contain 28% to 42% dead air. Running lightweight Silero VAD on-device or on edge workers strips silence before hitting Whisper or Deepgram, slashing billed minutes immediately.

2. Cache Repetitive TTS Phrases & Tier Expressive Voices

Greetings, confirmations, and IVR prompts account for 20–45% of spoken characters in production voice bots. Hashing static phrases to object storage and reserving ElevenLabs for high-value dialogue saves thousands monthly.
